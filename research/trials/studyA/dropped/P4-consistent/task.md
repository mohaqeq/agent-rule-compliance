# Clean up export files when a customer is deleted

Staff delete customers from the dashboard, one at a time or in bulk. Customers can generate CSV exports
(product lists and similar), and the finished files stay in our storage after the customer is gone.
Nobody can reach or download them any more, and we are paying to store them.

When a customer is deleted, also delete the export files that customer generated: both the export records
and the stored files. Files generated by other users must stay. This should work the same whether the
customer is deleted on its own or as part of a bulk delete.
